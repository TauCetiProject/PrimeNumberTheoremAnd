# PNT+ migration into Tau Ceti

Updated 2026-11-13. Dependencies: [Tau Ceti `62bbc05`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/62bbc0596dc3539d488232e4e924a338548ea0a9), [Mathlib `e8ba86f`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/e8ba86fa9744dd8d90f48c1752bc735f61a4ca81), stock Lean `v4.36.0-rc2`; exact pins are in [lake-manifest.json](lake-manifest.json). Approved specifications were checked against [TauCetiRoadmap `e1cd6a5`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/tree/e1cd6a5089db8179200d8379e1256c3f930cc4ff).

The retained fork has **86 library modules, one example and 36,240 lines of Lean**, including comments and blanks. At the user's request, IEANTN's 126-module tree, its dedicated Backlund/Fourier/Euler-bound support and LeanCert/PrimeCert dependencies are excluded. Its separate project is [teorth/IEANTN](https://github.com/teorth/IEANTN). This was a scope deletion, not a duplication or proof claim.

Every retained module received a source-by-source reuse pass. Subsequent dependency batches were checked through the retained import closure and selected relevant APIs. Current code uses Mathlib `WeakPNT` and native counting, growth, cutoff and reflection APIs. The cutoff/reflection adoption removes 75 lines and the specialized zeta-conjugation module, preserving the C² hypotheses and arbitrary complex inputs, including the zeta pole. Two further passes remove eight uniform-asymptotic wrappers in favor of Mathlib’s exact compact principal-filter bounds, product composition and fiberwise estimates, saving 101 lines while preserving supremum/infimum constants, empty parameter sets and bottom filters. The four genuinely broader ordered-group little-o statements remain. The separate log/power-limit wrapper and its module are removed: its caller uses Mathlib’s little-o quotient limit directly, preserving the full arbitrary-real-exponent claim and saving 24 lines. Cartan integrability now uses Mathlib’s meromorphic positive-log theorem and local power bounds, removing five duplicate integrability lemmas and 128 lines while recovering every original claim and preserving the exact constants. Another 30-line cleanup uses Mathlib’s norm inequality for the cosine bound and Tau Ceti’s exclusion of Gamma poles directly, deleting the duplicate exclusion helper with the original domain. Finite-product multiplicities now use Tau Ceti’s generic analytic-order sum, removing a 74-line induction while preserving arbitrary genus, repeated indices and the full extended-natural order statement. Hadamard natural-order proofs now use Mathlib’s product and centered-monomial rules directly, removing another 71 lines; the order-zero lemma at the origin no longer needs summability. The classical sinc-integral limit now comes directly from merged Tau Ceti, removing its exact local duplicate and ten lines; the quantitative tails, damped integral and weak inversion claims remain unchanged. These are bounded audits at the recorded revisions; upstream contribution and adoption continue.

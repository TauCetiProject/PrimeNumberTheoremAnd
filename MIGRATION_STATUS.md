The fork has **85 library modules, one example and 35,438 lines of Lean**, including comments and blanks. At the user's request, IEANTN's 126-module tree, its dedicated Backlund/Fourier/Euler-bound support and LeanCert/PrimeCert dependencies are excluded. Its separate project is [teorth/IEANTN](https://github.com/teorth/IEANTN). This was a scope deletion, not a duplication or proof claim. Migration into Tau Ceti also excludes `Unused/`: its four modules account for 2,728 lines. They remain in the fork and its build, but are not migration targets or roadmap blockers. The migration therefore covers **81 library modules, one example and 32,710 lines of Lean**.

Every module in the fork received a source-by-source reuse pass. Subsequent dependency batches were checked through its imports and selected relevant APIs. Current code uses Mathlib `WeakPNT` and native counting, growth, cutoff and reflection APIs. The cutoff/reflection adoption removes 75 lines and the specialized zeta-conjugation module, preserving the C² hypotheses and arbitrary complex inputs, including the zeta pole. Two further passes remove eight uniform-asymptotic wrappers in favor of Mathlib’s exact compact principal-filter bounds, product composition and fiberwise estimates, saving 101 lines while preserving supremum/infimum constants, empty parameter sets and bottom filters. The four genuinely broader ordered-group little-o statements remain. The separate log/power-limit wrapper and its module are removed: its caller uses Mathlib’s little-o quotient limit directly, preserving the full arbitrary-real-exponent claim and saving 24 lines. Cartan integrability now uses Mathlib’s meromorphic positive-log theorem and local power bounds, removing five duplicate integrability lemmas and 128 lines while recovering every original claim and preserving the exact constants. Another 30-line cleanup uses Mathlib’s norm inequality for the cosine bound and Tau Ceti’s exclusion of Gamma poles directly, deleting the duplicate exclusion helper with the original domain. Finite-product multiplicities now use Tau Ceti’s generic analytic-order sum, removing a 74-line induction while preserving arbitrary genus, repeated indices and the full extended-natural order statement. Hadamard natural-order proofs now use Mathlib’s product and centered-monomial rules directly, removing another 71 lines; the order-zero lemma at the origin no longer needs summability. The punctured-quotient bound now uses Mathlib’s bound for the norm of a continuous product on a neighborhood, removing another 37 lines while preserving arbitrary genus, functions and centers, including the zero function and empty divisor family. The complement-product nonvanishing proof now uses Mathlib’s convergence-to-zero and summable-majorant APIs, removing another 86 lines while preserving all theorem statements, arbitrary genus and the zero/empty-index cases. The classical sinc-integral limit now comes directly from merged Tau Ceti, removing its exact local duplicate and ten lines; the quantitative tails, damped integral and weak inversion claims remain unchanged. These are bounded audits at the recorded revisions; upstream contribution and adoption continue.

The log-power and polar-part adoption removed **269 lines**: three reciprocal-log-power integral wrappers now come from merged #39319 (32 lines), and the rectangle residue proofs use merged #39422’s polar-part constructor (237 lines). The original theorem statements and constants are preserved. The local limit definition of residue remains because it differs from the native meromorphic residue outside the stated simple-pole hypotheses.

The Gamma adoption removes the entire **112-line** critical-line decay module. Merged #39579 supplies the exact squared norm and the same √(2π) exponential bound at every real height; no alias or forwarding module is kept.

A further **111-line** reduction uses Mathlib’s logarithm-versus-power bound, intermediate-point existence and real/natural power commutation in the growth and dyadic-radius proofs.

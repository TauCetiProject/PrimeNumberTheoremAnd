# PNT+ migration into Tau Ceti

Audit: 2026-11-13. Source: [PNT+ `938912b`](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/tree/938912bbff637bdb18025f0cfd62db3939b63b0a); library: [Tau Ceti `be86813`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/be86813dc92e6bac8614c2c2dd34572145df09c0); approved specifications: [TauCetiRoadmap `7342e8f`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/tree/7342e8fdd). The dependency's actual pins are in [lake-manifest.json](lake-manifest.json).

This inventory covers the remaining PNT+ core. At the user's request, the entire
126-module IEANTN tree and its solely dependent `Backlund/ZeroCountCrude` support
module have been removed from this fork. IEANTN's separate project is
[teorth/IEANTN](https://github.com/teorth/IEANTN); its papers, tables, finite
certifications, Goldbach results and Erdős problem 392 are outside this migration.
The fork retains 92 library modules and one example, approximately 40,000 lines
including comments and blanks. The systematic Mathlib/Tau Ceti reuse audit is
still in progress; neither that phase nor upstream migration is complete.
Shared `Defs` now retains only the core Mertens function; IEANTN-only error-bound,
prime-gap and principal-value definitions are removed. Nth-prime results use
Mathlib's `Nat.nth Nat.Prime` directly. LeanCert and PrimeCert were needed only by the removed material and are no longer
dependencies. Lean and the Mathlib/Tau Ceti pins are unchanged.

For complete migration, genuinely additional specifications or extensions are still needed for:

- **Signed Cesàro Tauberian convergence:** the retained Hardy theorem assumes convergent Cesàro means and `|uₙ|≤A/n`. Newman and logarithmic Tauberian targets have different hypotheses; a narrow additional target is needed.
- **Fejér limits at jumps:** averaging the two one-sided limits needs an explicit target beyond the existing continuous-function uniform and `L¹` approximation targets.
- **Full Riemann–Stieltjes hypotheses:** extend [BanachAlgebras §2.1](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/blob/7342e8fdd/TauCetiRoadmap/BanachAlgebras/README.md#21-holomorphic-germs-and-riesz-projections) to arbitrary BV representatives and the source integration-by-parts theorem assuming existence of only one mesh integral. The native variation formula itself already exists; the detailed carrier and endpoint distinctions are below.

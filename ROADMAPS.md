# PNT+ migration into Tau Ceti

Audit: 2026-11-13. Source: [PNT+ `938912b`](https://github.com/AlexKontorovich/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/tree/938912bbff637bdb18025f0cfd62db3939b63b0a); library: [Tau Ceti `8b9f13d`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/8b9f13d9936343c962096355455995c9a128af39); approved specifications: [TauCetiRoadmap `7342e8f`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/tree/7342e8fdd). The dependency's actual pins are in [lake-manifest.json](lake-manifest.json).

This inventory covers the remaining PNT+ core. At the user's request, the entire
126-module IEANTN tree and its dedicated `Backlund/ZeroCountCrude`,
`FourierDecay` and `EulerMascheroniBounds` support modules have been removed.
The last two have only IEANTN mathematical consumers in the original source;
the numerical Euler bounds were introduced for the explicit `li(2)` calculation.
IEANTN's separate project is
[teorth/IEANTN](https://github.com/teorth/IEANTN); its papers, tables, finite
certifications, Goldbach results and Erdős problem 392 are outside this migration.
The fork retains 88 library modules and one example, 37,227 lines
including comments and blanks across the library and example. The initial
source-by-source Mathlib/Tau Ceti reuse pass was completed at the original pins. The forward dependency update below opens another adoption pass. Upstream migration remains open.
All retained mathematical declarations now pass the standard-axiom audit.
Shared `Defs` now retains only the core Mertens function; IEANTN-only error-bound,
prime-gap and principal-value definitions are removed. Nth-prime results use
Mathlib's `Nat.nth Nat.Prime` directly. LeanCert and PrimeCert were needed only by the removed material and are no longer
dependencies. Lean remains stock `v4.36.0-rc2`; Tau Ceti advances to `8b9f13d` with its matching Mathlib `e8ba86f`.

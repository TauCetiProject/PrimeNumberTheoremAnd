# PNT+ migration into Tau Ceti

Updated 2026-11-13. Dependencies: [Tau Ceti `62bbc05`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/tree/62bbc0596dc3539d488232e4e924a338548ea0a9), [Mathlib `e8ba86f`](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/tree/e8ba86fa9744dd8d90f48c1752bc735f61a4ca81), stock Lean `v4.36.0-rc2`; exact pins are in [lake-manifest.json](lake-manifest.json). Approved specifications were checked against [TauCetiRoadmap `5907be9`](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCetiRoadmap/tree/5907be9ca9abda0213fa76f4345802711e619452).

The retained fork has **87 library modules, one example and 36,567 lines of Lean**, including comments and blanks. At the user's request, IEANTN's 126-module tree, its dedicated Backlund/Fourier/Euler-bound support and LeanCert/PrimeCert dependencies are excluded. Its separate project is [teorth/IEANTN](https://github.com/teorth/IEANTN). This was a scope deletion, not a duplication or proof claim.

Every retained module received a source-by-source reuse pass. Subsequent dependency batches were checked through the retained import closure and selected relevant APIs. Current code uses Mathlib `WeakPNT` and native counting, growth, cutoff and reflection APIs. The cutoff/reflection adoption removes 75 lines and the specialized zeta-conjugation module, preserving the C² hypotheses and arbitrary complex inputs, including the zeta pole. Two further passes remove eight uniform-asymptotic wrappers in favor of Mathlib’s exact compact principal-filter bounds, product composition and fiberwise estimates, saving 101 lines while preserving supremum/infimum constants, empty parameter sets and bottom filters. The four genuinely broader ordered-group little-o statements remain. The classical sinc-integral limit now comes directly from merged Tau Ceti, removing its exact local duplicate and ten lines; the quantitative tails, damped integral and weak inversion claims remain unchanged. These are bounded audits at the recorded revisions; upstream contribution and adoption continue.

## Verification and retained distinctions

Current source passes the complete library build, blueprint indexing and rectangle example. All **3,265 compiled library declarations** pass the standard-axiom and declaration-ownership audit; there are no remaining admissions. The full complex Ramanujan, alternate ζ³ and Liouville identities are proved. Documentation now states the actual rectangle-meromorphy assumptions, including removable singularities and listed boundary points, and corrects the pole-order factor in the residue formula; Lean declarations are unchanged. The earlier [cutoff/reflection refactor](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/commit/3e0a6432b6ef6e3231d8d177a90882a094e227d0) also passes [GitHub CI](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/PrimeNumberTheoremAnd/actions/runs/43458298482).

Native finite-order APIs replace local nonnegative-order work; the local negative-order convention remains distinct (native `OrderLE` admits constant 2, the local predicate excludes it). Weak residue/product statements lack meromorphic hypotheses and remain. StrongPNT already uses native local factorization, while its flexible-radius estimate allows radii excluded by the native bound. W21 retains C²/integrable-derivative hypotheses; Schwartz/C∞ density is not an exact replacement. Unique proved `Unused/` results are retained. `HoffsteinLockhart` is blueprint prose, and the simproc belongs to human-owned tooling rather than Tau Ceti mathematics.

[Dirichlet/Hurwitz growth #37557](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/37557) and [vertical Stirling #38098](https://github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti/pull/38098) have merged into the pinned dependency.
